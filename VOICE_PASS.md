# Final voice pass

The structure can be edited by AI. The autobiography cannot.

Before the Academy application is submitted, every project page gets one spoken pass from Nate. The source of truth is what Nate actually says out loud, not the current draft.

For each project, answer these without trying to sound impressive:

1. **What is it?** Explain it to a smart friend who has never seen it.
2. **Why did you start it?** What actually happened or bothered you?
3. **What did you personally build?** Be concrete.
4. **What was harder than you expected?** Include the part that went wrong or confused you.
5. **What changed while you were building it?** What did you stop doing, rebuild, or change your mind about?
6. **What exists now?** Live product, code, users, unfinished pieces, real result.
7. **What did you learn that changed the next thing you built?**

Editing rule: cut repetition, fix grammar only when it gets in the way, preserve Nate's sentence structure and specific examples. Do not add founder language, lessons he did not say, or generic lines about passion / impact / innovation.
